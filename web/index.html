Quarterly income tax tracker and BIR Form 1701Q figure preparer for Philippine freelancers and virtual assistants

Record what comes in through the year. This works out the cumulative tax due at the end of each quarter under the regime you have elected, credits what the earlier quarters already paid, and lays the figures out ready to transcribe. It files nothing and it sends nothing anywhere.

     

1 · What you elected for this year

This tool does not choose for you. The election is made on registration or on the first quarterly return of the year and, under NIRC sec. 24(A)(2)(b), it cannot be changed for that year. Deciding between the 8% flat rate and the graduated rates is a separate job, and a separate tool does it.
